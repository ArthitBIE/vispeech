# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thai learners of all levels practicing pronunciation in a self-study context. They open the app aiming to improve their spoken Thai, track progress over time, and get immediate feedback on mouth shape and speech accuracy. Sessions are short and focused — practice a word, get a score, move on.

## Product Purpose

vispeech helps Thai learners improve pronunciation accuracy and build speaking confidence through real-time analysis of mouth shape and speech. Success is a learner who can see measurable progress, feels encouraged to keep practicing, and eventually pronounces words naturally without the app's help.

## Brand Personality

Playful, energetic, encouraging — like a supportive tutor who makes practice fun. Warm without being saccharine, confident without being corporate. The tone in Thai is friendly and casual ("ลองออกเสียงคำนี้!" not formal instruction).

## Anti-references

Cluttered, dense UIs. The practice flow should feel spacious and focused — one word, one attempt, clear feedback. Avoid dashboard-overload where the learner feels buried in data before they've even started.

## Design Principles

- **Focus on the practice moment.** The core interaction is looking at yourself on camera and speaking a word. Everything else (stats, history, navigation) is secondary. The UI gets out of the way when it matters most.
- **Progress at a glance.** When the learner checks their dashboard, they should immediately understand where they stand — not wade through tables and filters.
- **Feedback that teaches.** Scores alone don't improve pronunciation. Feedback is specific, encouraging, and actionable — whether the user scored 30 or 90.
- **Spaces not surfaces.** Prioritize breathing room and clarity over information density. One clear action per screen, not a page of options.
- **Personality in the details.** A playful brand lives in micro-copy, transitions, and empty states — not in decorative graphics that get in the way.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web best practices: semantic HTML, keyboard navigable, sufficient color contrast. Thai language throughout (UI strings, labels, feedback) with locale-aware date formatting.
